# ADR-0007: An original Gila monster mark, a cover of our own design, and a landing page that is the book's home

## Status

Accepted, 2026-11-05.

## Context

The author wants the eye-catch of a well-known publisher's animal covers without using anything that is theirs, a public page that is the book's canonical home and carries his authorship, a temporary opening that pitches the book before it exists, and the pitch in several formats for handing out.

## Decision

1. **The mark is a Gila monster**, drawn as original bold flat line art in two colours (ink and orange on sand), in `Book/images/gila-monster.svg`. It is the book's and the imprint's mark and is copyright Stewart Rogers, all rights reserved; it is not under the book's Creative Commons licence.
2. **What the cover must never have**: an engraving, woodcut, stipple or any nineteenth-century print style for the animal; a white cover with a coloured band across the top; any publisher's typeface, series name or styled "Cookbook" mark. The cover is `Book/images/cover.svg`: sand ground, heavy sans title, the mark large, author and imprint in our own placement.
3. **The landing page** at `Book/site/template.html` is built by `build/site.py` from `book.yml`, the cover and the first recipe's rendered body, so the one-file identity drives it. It opens with a preview pitch, dated, that is replaced by the real front matter at the first edition. It carries the author's name, role and the two programs. It makes no claim a recipe is verified when it is not.
4. **It is published on GitHub Pages** from the Surly repository by `.github/workflows/book.yml` on every push that touches `Book/`, at a stable address under the author's name. That is the canonical home the rights strategy calls for.
5. **The pitch is distributed from `Book/dist/`**: page, Portable Document Format, Markdown, plain text, and the cover as PNG and SVG, all generated by `site.py` and committed so they can be sent without building anything.

## Consequences

- The cover will change; the mark is meant to last. Anything that reuses the mark reuses the author's property.
- The page's pitch section is temporary by design and says so.
