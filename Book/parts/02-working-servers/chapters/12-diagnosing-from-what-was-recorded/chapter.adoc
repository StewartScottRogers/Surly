== Diagnosing from What Was Recorded

Each recipe answers a question about something that has already happened, such as which account wrote a file or which listener a client came in on, from what `surl` recorded at the time: its verbose log, its events and its recorded exchanges. The record is read after the run, once it has caught up with it.
