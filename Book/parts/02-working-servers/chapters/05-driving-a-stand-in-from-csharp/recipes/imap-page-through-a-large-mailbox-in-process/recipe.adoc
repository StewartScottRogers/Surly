=== Paging Through a Large Mailbox

==== Problem

Your C# test checks a mailbox holding hundreds of messages on an in-process stand-in server. You want to page through them with `ListMessagesAsync`, `afterUid` and `HasMore`, seeing every message once even if mail arrives or is deleted while you walk.

==== Solution

Ask for a page of at most 100 messages after the last unique identifier (UID) seen, starting from 0, and stop when `HasMore` is false:

==== Discussion

`SurlServerOptions` is `surl`{apos}s command line as properties (<<surl-readme>>). `ListenUrls` holds one `imap://` URL on port 0, for Internet Message Access Protocol (IMAP) clients, and the system picks a free port; `MailDomains` is `--mail-domain`, and `Mailboxes` is `--mailbox`, Ann{apos}s address and password. `server.Mail.DeliverAsync` seeds 250 order notices straight into her INBOX, as UIDs 1 to 250.

`server.Mail.ListMessagesAsync` takes the owner, the mailbox, `afterUid` and a limit, and answers a `SurlMessagePage`: `Messages`, at most the limit of them, in UID order, each a `SurlMessageSummary` with its `Uid`, size, flags and the date the store took it in; and `HasMore`, whether any follow. It reads the store directly, with no client and no login. The walk starts with `afterUid` 0 and passes the last UID of each page to the next call. Pages of 100 give UIDs 1 to 100, 101 to 200 and 201 to 250, and the third says `HasMore` false. Every message is seen once, in UID order, and `ReadMessageAsync` reads the last back as a `SurlMailMessage`: order 250. `HasMore` is exact. With 200 messages the second page is full and already says false, so the walk never asks for an empty page.

The walk goes by UID, not by position, and that is what keeps it honest while the mailbox changes. A UID names one message for good, and a new message always gets a larger one than any before it (<<rfc9051>>). A message delivered after the first page becomes UID 251 and turns up on the last page. When Ann{apos}s mail client deletes UID 10, already seen, after the first page, the next page still starts at UID 101. The program plays that client with `Curl.Api`: a `CurlClient` and two `CurlRequest`s on the INBOX URL with `CurlAuthenticationOptions` for Ann{apos}s login, whose `CustomRequest` is the IMAP command, `UID STORE 10 +FLAGS (\Deleted)` and then `EXPUNGE`; each `CurlResponse` body is the server{apos}s answer, which the program discards. Counting by position instead, a walk that skipped the first 100 would now start at UID 102: the 101st message has moved, and UID 101 would be missed.

The limit is 1 to 1000. A page of 1001 is refused with `SurlReadRefusedException`, `The limit must be 1 to 1000.`; the store does not cut it down for you.
