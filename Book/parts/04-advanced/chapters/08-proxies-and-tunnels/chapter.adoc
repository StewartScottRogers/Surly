== Proxies and Tunnels

The recipes here put a proxy between a client and its server: `surl --proxy` forwards requests and opens `CONNECT` tunnels on your own machine, so a test can show that a client really goes through its proxy. By default the proxy reaches only loopback origins, and each recipe says what widening that costs.
