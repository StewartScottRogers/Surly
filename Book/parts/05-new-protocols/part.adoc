= New Protocols

The schemes the Curl port adds and upstream curl refuses, and the `surl` servers that answer them: object stores, message brokers, remote calls and streams, lookups and data stores. Each is judged by its own specification, not by curl. These recipes move forward into the tier their difficulty puts them in once both programs have released them and they are verified on three platforms.
